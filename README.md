# 2026 Argentina Ag-Robotics & Precision AI Visibility Index (`v.2026.11`)

Empirical study measuring the Generative Engine Optimization (GEO) baseline, Share of Recommendation (SOR), and citation leakage across enterprise agricultural robotics in Latin America.

## 🏛️ Benchmark Overview
* **Lead Architect:** [Fabio Yocco](https://github.com/fabivsy)
* **Protocol Standard:** FixGEO Protocol (`v.2026.11`)
* **Engines Evaluated:** ChatGPT Search (OAI-SearchBot), Perplexity Pro, Genspark, Google AI Overviews.
* **On-Chain Registry:** `FixGeoAuditRegistry.sol` on Ethereum Sepolia (`0x3b2Cd2c620976826F1C98CDdBb20C40AED6c0B6B`).

## 📊 Summary Findings
* **68% Specification Hallucination Rate:** Traditional RAG parsers chunking multi-page technical PDF brochures drop tabular rows and misattribute critical yield metrics.
* **The "Cotizar" Pricing Trap:** 85% of evaluated OEM platforms hide commercial figures behind quote buttons, causing LLMs to omit them in favor of transparent competitors.
* **Citation Sovereignty:** Up to 80% of brand citations were captured by aggregators and dealers rather than the canonical OEM domain prior to edge SSR and JSON-LD remediation.

Detailed benchmark datasets are stored in `benchmark-data.json`.
